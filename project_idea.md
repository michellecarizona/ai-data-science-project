My project will investigate how different types of meteorological conditions affect PM10 air pollution levels in Phoenix, Arizona. I want to determine which condition is most strongly asscosciated with higher levels of dust and particle matter in the air. 
This topic is important because dust pollution can siginificantly reduce air quality and can create major health and environmental concerns in the Phoenix area. 
I expect to use historical weather data and PM10 air-quality measurements for this project.
